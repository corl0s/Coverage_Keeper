# Product: Coverage Keeper

Coverage Keeper is an insurer-agnostic AI member-retention layer. It flags health-plan members at risk of losing coverage over paperwork, reaches them in their language, finds the compliance/exemption pathway their documents support, catches missing evidence, drafts a cited packet and third-party request letters, and verifies every claim.

- Demo program: the MassHealth (Massachusetts **Medicaid**, not Medicare) community-engagement requirement starting Jan 1, 2027.
- Hackathon project: Hackachusetts 2026, BU DS+X.
- Outcomes per case: **Retain**, **Recover** or **Transition**.
